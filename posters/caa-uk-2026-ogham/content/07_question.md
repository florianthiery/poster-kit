The corpus marks 34 records as doubtful, 21 of them British, and 20 stones have no coordinate at all. Do these records reflect a material fact, or the history of who looked at them and how?

A weathered stone is a fact about the object. A reading that nobody can check is a fact about the record. The editions mark both kinds of doubt with care; this poster follows that care from the TEI edition to a weighted statement.
