Two open Python pipelines read the same TEI/EpiDoc editions of the OG(H)AM project.

- **Axis 1, structure:** a class-based crosswalk into CIDOC CRM and CRMtex.
- **Axis 2, doubt:** every competing reading becomes a weighted statement in the Academic Meta Tool (AMT).
- Each reading names its editor, so a weight can be traced to its source.
