Figures and photographs of this poster. Portraits go into `img/people/`.
Logos used by several posters belong in `../../shared/logos/`.

## Figures (generated)

`map-corpus.png`, `ladder-all.png`, `gigha-readings.png`, `gigha-graph.png`, `gigha-reasoning.png` and `gigha-photo.png`
are drawn by the scripts in `../figures/` (see the README there). They are checked in because their inputs live in other
repositories. The map is drawn on Natural Earth land polygons (public domain, <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>).
