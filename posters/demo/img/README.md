# Images

Put figures and photographs here and reference them from `poster.yaml` with an `image` part
(`src: img/<file>`). PNG, JPG and SVG work; SVG stays vector in the PDF (basic shapes, paths and groups;
no embedded fonts or filters). Photos of people go into `img/people/` (`people` part). Logos used by more than one poster go into `shared/logos/` (`header.logos`, `supporters` and `logos` parts).

The sample files (`sample-figure.svg`, `sample-photo.png`, `people/sample-person-*.png` and the logos in `shared/logos/`) are generated
placeholders without any rights attached; delete them when you replace the demo poster.

Aim for at least 150 dpi at the printed size for raster images: a photograph that fills a 400 mm wide card
should be about 2400 px wide.
